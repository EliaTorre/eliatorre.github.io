Personal macOS workspace

Your work, together on your Mac.

Alfred brings documents, tasks, recordings, email and calendars into a locally stored workspace.

Alfred is a desktop application for personal use. Its local features work without a hosted Alfred account. Email and AI are optional online connections; they are not required to organize local files or transcribe recordings.

Documents and recordings

Organize files, edit documents, annotate PDFs, manage tasks and transcribe audio locally. Workspace content and conversation history are saved on your Mac.

Email and calendars

Connect Gmail to read, search and organize messages, manage drafts, and send replies and attachments. Optional Google Calendar access lets you display calendars, manage events and respond to invitations.

Why Alfred requests Google access

Google sign-in identifies the account you choose. Gmail access provides Alfred’s email features. Calendar access is requested separately when you enable calendar and invitation management. Credentials are stored in macOS Keychain; Alfred uses Google’s APIs directly from your Mac.

Optional AI assistance

You can connect your signed-in Codex or Claude Code installation, an API provider, or a local model server. When you use AI, prompts and the workspace content needed for the requested task can be sent to that provider. In-app AI changes require review in Alfred.

This site describes a personal desktop installation. It does not host your mailbox or workspace, provide a public sign-up service, or offer an app download.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for the data accessed, storage, optional sharing, and deletion controls. For questions about your Google connection, contact the application owner using the support email shown on Google’s Alfred consent screen.
