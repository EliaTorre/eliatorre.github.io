Your data in Alfred.

This policy describes Alfred, the personal macOS workspace identified on the Google consent screen as “Alfred.” It covers the desktop app’s Google connection and this informational site.

Information Alfred accesses

  • Account identity: your Google account identifier and email address identify and reconnect the correct account.
  • Gmail: message bodies, headers, sender and recipient details, labels, thread information, drafts and attachments support reading, searching, organizing and sending email.
  • Optional Google Calendar: your calendar list and events, including titles, descriptions, times, locations, recurrence and attendees, support calendar display, event management and invitation responses.
  • Workspace content: files you import or create, audio and transcripts, tasks, comments, settings, local search data and agent conversations are stored in the workspace you choose.

How data is used

Alfred uses this information to provide the features you request: synchronizing and displaying your mail and calendars, searching cached content, composing and sending email, managing events, and assisting with workspace tasks. Google Calendar editing is optional. Google permissions are requested through Google’s consent flow; Alfred does not ask for or store your Google password.

Storage and security

The desktop app stores its workspace, cached messages and calendar events, drafts, search indexes, conversation history and backups on your Mac. OAuth credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Alfred communicates with Google’s APIs over HTTPS. Authorized local MCP clients connect through an authenticated loopback service.

Alfred does not operate a hosted workspace or mailbox backend for this personal installation. Local workspace files are not separately encrypted by Alfred; your Mac’s user permissions, disk protection and backup settings matter. Copies you export, back up or place in a synchronized folder follow the controls of that destination. Workspace backups exclude Keychain credentials.

When information leaves your Mac

  • Google: authentication and mail/calendar operations communicate with Google. Sending a message, event invitation or response delivers its contents to the recipients you select.
  • Optional AI: if you use Codex, Claude Code or another configured online model, Alfred sends your prompt and the task context retrieved by the model’s permitted tools to that provider. This may include email, calendar or document content. In-app writes require your approval. The provider’s account settings and policies govern its processing and retention; a local model can be used instead.
  • Authorized external clients: MCP clients you connect may read workspace content and apply actions according to their own permissions and approval settings. An external client may send returned content to its configured model provider.
  • Your exports and support requests: you control exported files and anything you choose to share for support.

Alfred does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Alfred does not use Google user data to train a general-purpose AI model. Any optional transfer must serve the user-facing feature you request.

Google API Limited Use

Alfred’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting, retention and deletion

Disconnect an account in Alfred’s Connections settings to remove its locally stored authorization and stop future synchronization. Disconnecting preserves cached messages, drafts and calendar data for offline use; it does not delete your mailbox or calendar at Google. You can also revoke Alfred’s authorization in your Google Account’s third-party connections.

Local workspace content and backups remain until you remove them. Alfred’s storage controls can clear disposable caches. To remove all local workspace data, first export anything you need, stop Alfred and its background service, and remove the workspace and any copies or backups you no longer want. This removes other content in that workspace too. Find the active workspace location in Alfred’s Workspace settings. Removing local files does not remove data already sent to recipients, stored at Google, or submitted to an AI provider.

This website

These pages contain no analytics, advertising scripts, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website never receives your Alfred workspace or Google OAuth credentials.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email displayed on Google’s Alfred consent screen. This policy will be updated if Alfred’s data practices change. The date at the top identifies this version.
